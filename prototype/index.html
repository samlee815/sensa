How a session works

One simple choice.
A better state of you.

Five to ten minutes, designed to fit between the meeting and the moment that matters. Conversation is optional; a tap is always enough.

  1. 01

    Sense

    Ear-worn EEG and PPG read brain and pulse signals, combined with a quick note on how you feel.

  2. 02

    Choose

    Pick a target state. Sensa offers an informed suggestion, and the choice is always yours.

  3. 03

    Shift

    Gentle ear stimulation and adaptive sound begin, with the change shown in real time.

  4. 04

    Learn

    Before-and-after signals and your feedback shape the next session.
